# AI reel credits

These mock reels (MP4 + JPG poster) are generated by `scripts/mock-media/render-reels.swift` from the
vehicle photos in `public/mock-media/vehicles/`. Each reel `{key}.mp4` uses the photos of the set listed
under the same key in `scripts/mock-media/reel-sets.json`.

The source photos come from Wikimedia Commons; their authors and licences are listed in
[`../vehicles/ATTRIBUTION.md`](../vehicles/ATTRIBUTION.md). The reels are adaptations (cropped, panned
and composited) and are shared under the same licence as their source photos.
